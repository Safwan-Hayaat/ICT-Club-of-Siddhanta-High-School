The History of ICT at Mirpur Siddhanta High School

Safwan Hayaat

Content

  • Introduction
  • History

Introduction

In Mirpur Siddhanta High School, the usage of ICT and the history of clubs have been brief but somewhat eventful. In 2013, the first ICT textbooks in Bangladesh were issued by the NCTB. Since then, the awareness about ICT in Bangladesh has slowly grown. As more and more people learned about the usage and conveniency of ICT, it became a part of their daily lives. Today ICT is integrated into every corner of civilization. Mirpur Siddhanta High School is no exception, as the ICT club has became an important club in the school and ICT has become an important tool in the development of the school.

History

On the 28th of September, 1997, the Administrative building was constructed in the Mirpur Siddhanta High School campus. At the time, it was the biggest building in our school. Today, it houses the ICT lab on its 2nd floor. As far as we know from different sources, in the mid to early 2010s, a classroom in the Administrative building was converted in the ICT lab. Back then Bangladesh lacked the proper foundations for digital accomadations. There was a lack of proper knowlege on ICT at that time. Subsequently, the lab lacked many basic resources to function properly. However, in between 2020-2023, during a national government project to develop the nation, the governemnt improved the lab with wi-fi connection, better laptops, a TV, and an overall improvement to the room. This improvement made the lab eligible for basic teaching.

In September 2024, Mr. Mostafizur Rahman sir became the current headmaster of our school. He planned to create clubs in the school as a way to improve the school with extracurriculars. After months of planning, that plan was brought to life in the 2025 academic year, when 12 clubs were created in the school. Each club was split into 2 parts, morning and day shift respectively. Starting in 2025, all 12 clubs, including the science club, English language club, debate club, culture club, sports club and last but not least, the ICT club started their activities. In January 2025, the first executive committee was established. 7 members were chosen, each with their own roles. Sakhawat Hossain sir and the late Anowar Hossain sir were chosen as the club's teachers. In February of the same year, the men's ICT club became one of the first clubs to initiate long-term activities. As the club started its weekly classes on each Thursday. With the addition of clubs, the first Youth Fair took place in the school on 20 February, 2025. The Youth Fair, also known as the Science Fair or ICT Fair; is an annual intra-school competition between clubs to create the best projects. Each year prizes are gizen between each shift both individually and collectively. The fair helps increase the creativity of students, allowing them to create projects of their own will. After rigorous work from members of the club from all classes, the ICT club successfully claimed 2nd place at the fair. This was the first major win for the club just months after the creation of it.

To celebrate the Bengali New Year of 1432, the 1432 Boishakhi Mela was hosted in Mirpur Siddhanta High School on April 14th. But starting from that year, clubs played a major role in the event. On the first day of summer, all clubs gathered together and claimed stalls in the school campus. It was each clubs task to decorate their stalls and sell different products. On that day, each student used their skills to create and sell different types of products. Some generated thousands of Taka in profits, while others lost hundreds.

The 2025 Academic year was a decent year for clubs. Being the first year after clubs were created, it is true that the year was not perfect. Most clubs did not operate or have activities outside of the mandatory events. Even clubs that did have activities had many members who did not participate. Since it was the first year with clubs, the year was obviously not the greatest. Despite the problems, the original idea of improving the school with extracurriculars did work, as clubs inspired and aided many people to learn and do things beyond expectations.

Going into the 2026 Academic year, the stakes were different. Clubs activities were more emphasised and clubs started more regular activities. Meetings were held between club leaders and teachers about future events. Rezowan Islam sir and Shariful Islam sir became incharge of the club beside Sakhawat sir. In February, the 2026 executive committee was created and lab classes resumed on Wednesday. The 1433 Boishakhi Mela was hosted as usual and we reach the present day. As we look into the near future, the future of our school comes to mind. The past couple years has been important for the schools development. So has it been for the ICT club. It is our greatest dream that; one day, Bangladesh and our school will catch up to this modern world and ICT will be used to its fullest and best extent.

From yesterday, today was shaped. And from today, we can change the future. So it is important that, instead of thinking of what we could have done in the past, or overthinking the future, we should act now and change the course of the world forever.
